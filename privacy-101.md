## Android privacy manual

#### Remove all "diagnostics"

#### Google services and ads

Settings -> google -> google services -> all services
Ads -> delete your advertising id
Personalize using shared data -> disable everything, this leaks your on device data to google
Usage and diagnostics -> off, you dont want to send anything to google

Ads
Settings -> privacy -> ad services
disable everything there


Also check your phone for any "user experience" programs
for me its in settings -> privacy -> user experience program
Here I turn off user experience program
Send diagnostic data automatically

Those are all just trackers and you dont want that
